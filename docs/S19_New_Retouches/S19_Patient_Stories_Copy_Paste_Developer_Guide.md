##### **ACTION** 

Keep the existing disclaimer, but replace/expand the disclaimer block so it contains all of the approved wording below. 

##### **EXACT COPY TO PASTE** 

#### Patient Testimonial Disclaimer 

The experiences shared on this page are personal accounts from individual S19 patients and clients. Individual experiences and outcomes vary, and the results described should not be considered typical, guaranteed or expected for every patient. 

Testimonials do not establish that an S19 product, herbal formulation or treatment program caused a reported improvement or outcome. They are shared for informational purposes and should not be interpreted as medical advice or as a substitute for individualized medical assessment. 

Some testimonials may have been lightly edited for spelling, grammar, length or clarity, or translated from another language, while preserving the patient's intended meaning. 

Patients taking prescribed medication should not stop or reduce their medication based on another patient's experience. Medication changes should be made only with appropriate clinical supervision. 

##### **DO NOT CHANGE** 

Do not place medical conclusions inside individual patient quotations.

- Do not remove the consent/privacy statement. 

- Do not imply that patients should discontinue conventional treatment.
