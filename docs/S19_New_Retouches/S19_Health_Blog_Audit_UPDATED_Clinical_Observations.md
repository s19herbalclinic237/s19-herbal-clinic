## **1. IMPORTANT EDITORIAL DISTINCTION** 

S19 website content should clearly separate three different types of information: 

A. Published scientific evidence - statements supported by appropriate external scientific or clinical sources. 

B. S19 documented clinical observations - outcomes S19 has observed in individual patients and documented in confidential patient records and laboratory results. 

C. Patient testimonials - the patient's own description of his or her experience. 

Do not automatically convert an individual S19 observation or testimonial into a universal statement that S19 treatment has been proven to cure every patient with that condition. 

## **2. EXACT WEBSITE COPY - S19 CLINICAL EXPERIENCE** 

Clinical Experience at S19 

In our clinical practice, we have observed patients with previously documented infections, including hepatitis B and herpes, who later obtained negative follow-up test results after completing individualized S19 care. 

These observations are based on patient records and laboratory results maintained confidentially by the clinic. Because protecting patient privacy is important to us, individual laboratory records are not published without the patient's explicit consent. 

Individual outcomes can vary. A negative follow-up result must also be interpreted according to the specific infection, the laboratory marker or test performed, the timing of testing, other treatments received and the duration of follow-up. These clinical observations should therefore not be interpreted as a guarantee that every patient will achieve the same outcome.

## **5. PATIENT PRIVACY - EXACT COPY** 

Why Doesn't S19 Publish Every Patient's Laboratory Results? 

Patient confidentiality comes first. S19 maintains clinical and laboratory information as private health information and does not publish identifiable patient records simply to prove a treatment outcome. 

Where a patient has not explicitly consented to publication, their laboratory reports, identity and personal medical details remain confidential. S19 may describe clinical experience in a general or anonymized manner where appropriate, without exposing information that identifies an individual patient.

## **7. BLOG-WIDE RULE FOR S19 OUTCOME STATEMENTS** 

When an outcome comes from S19's own patient records, use language such as: 

Preferred: 'S19 has observed...' / 'S19 has documented individual cases...' / 'In our clinical experience...' / 'Some patients in our care have subsequently obtained...' 

Avoid converting this into: 'S19 cures...' / 'Our treatment guarantees...' / 'This treatment clears the infection in everyone...' / 'All patients become negative...' / fixed cure timelines. 

If a numerical success rate is ever published, it must come from a defined patient group with a clear denominator, outcome definition, follow-up period and appropriate analysis - not an informal estimate.
