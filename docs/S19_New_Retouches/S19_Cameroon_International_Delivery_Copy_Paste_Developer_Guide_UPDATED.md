## **07. DEVELOPER RULES** 

- Spell Bertoua exactly as shown. 

- Retain 'and other cities across Cameroon' so customers outside the named cities do not assume S19 cannot deliver to them. 

- International examples should include USA, Canada, UK and UAE, plus selected African countries and other supported destinations. 

- Do not state or imply that S19 delivers to every country worldwide. 

- Use 'supported destinations' because shipping availability can depend on destination and shipping conditions. 

- Keep Cameroon delivery and international delivery visually distinct. 

- Do not invent fixed delivery charges, courier companies, customs fees or delivery times. 

- Customers should contact S19 to confirm international availability and applicable shipping charges. 

- Keep wording consistent on desktop and mobile.
