# **S19-CARE** 

Website donation page | Developer implementation brief | S19 Herbal Clinic 

## **1. Main donation page - approved short message** 

### **Your Kindness Can Help Someone Heal.** 

Many village parents and families living in poverty cannot afford the healthcare they need. Some struggle to pay for S19 treatment plans or medical tests. Others cannot afford transportation, accommodation, or even mobile data for follow-up. 

### **Through S19-CARE, your donation can help.** 

Your gift can support treatment plans, essential tests, transport to the clinic, accommodation, and mobile data so patients can stay in touch with our care team. It can also support other practical needs during care. 

No donation is too small. Together, we can help more families access care. 

**Give Hope. Support a Family. Help Save Lives.** 

## **2. Donation purposes** 

- **Patient Assistance Fund:** Helps eligible low-income patients and families with treatment plans, tests, transport, accommodation, mobile data, and related care needs. 

- **S19 Development Fund:** Helps improve clinic equipment, infrastructure, training, education, outreach, and healthcare services. 

- **General Donation:** Used where support is most needed, in line with the published donation policy.
