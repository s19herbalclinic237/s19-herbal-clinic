### **SECTION 3 - S19 PATIENT PATHWAY** 

Important change: Replace the current simplified "Book -> Protocol -> Dispatch -> Follow-up" presentation with a pathway that reflects the actual S19 clinical process. 

Display prominently: 

CONSULT -> INVESTIGATE -> UNDERSTAND -> EDUCATE -> ADDRESS & SUPPORT -> MONITOR 

Card 1 - Consult: Detailed consultation covering concerns, symptoms, medical history, previous treatments and current medications. 

Card 2 - Investigate: Review existing laboratory tests, scans and medical records and, where appropriate, request further investigations. 

Card 3 - Understand: Bring the findings together to better understand the condition and contributing factors. 

Card 4 - Educate: Explain what has been identified, why it matters and how the patient can participate in care. 

Card 5 - Address & Support: Develop an individualized plan that may combine herbal medicine, nutrition, lifestyle interventions and appropriate supportive therapies. 

Card 6 - Monitor: Follow progress, review relevant measurements/laboratory findings and adjust the plan when appropriate.

### **SECTION 5 - SCIENCE-BASED HERBALISM** 

Heading: Science-Based Herbalism Subheading: Natural Does Not Automatically Mean Safe 

Herbs contain biologically active compounds. They can produce physiological effects, interact with medications and may not be appropriate for every person. Responsible herbal care therefore requires more than simply choosing a plant for a symptom. 

Display as visual keywords: 

Pharmacology | Phytochemistry | Dosage | Contraindications | Toxicity | Herb-Drug Interactions | Existing Medications | Laboratory Findings | Individual Health Status 

Closing copy: We combine traditional knowledge with modern scientific understanding and available evidence to make herbal care more responsible and individualized. Button: LEARN HOW WE PRACTICE HERBAL MEDICINE 

### **SECTION 6 - NOT ANTI-CONVENTIONAL TREATMENT** 

New credibility section. This should be visually prominent but concise. 

Heading: S19 Is Not Anti-Conventional Treatment 

S19 Herbal Clinic recognizes the essential role of conventional healthcare. Pharmaceutical drugs, surgery, emergency medicine, hospitalization and specialist care can be necessary and lifesaving. 

Our approach is not about choosing "natural versus conventional." It is about understanding the individual and using appropriate strategies responsibly. 

Where conventional treatment is necessary, herbal and lifestyle support should complement - not delay or replace - appropriate medical care. 

Safety line: Patients should never stop or reduce prescribed medication without appropriate clinical supervision.
